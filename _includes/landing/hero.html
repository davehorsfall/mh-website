What is Play Therapy and how does it work?

Play therapy provides a safe space for children to explore their thoughts and feelings through creative play- based experiences as the child's natural language. I work with compassion to build a supportive therapeutic relationship based on trust and understanding, where the child is free to express themselves and make choices. Children work creatively, using a play and creative arts toolkit including drawing/painting, clay, puppets, role play and storytelling to process feelings and any unresolved trauma. In this protected space, the child gains insight into their own world, at their own pace, in a safe way.

I follow a holistic approach to understanding the child, taking into account their developmental stage, relationships, environment, and emotions, while working closely with parents and teachers to meet the child's mental health needs and strengthen outcomes. I follow an integrated theoretical approach, which offers child-centred 1:1 or group clinical interventions, tailored to meet the developmental needs and family context of the child.
