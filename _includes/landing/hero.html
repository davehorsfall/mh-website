What is Play Therapy and how does it work?

Play Therapy provides a safe space for children to explore thoughts and feelings within a trusting therapeutic relationship. Children work creatively using a toolkit promoting play and a creative arts including drawing/painting, clay, and puppets. role-play and storytelling. Children are able to use play as their natural language to communicate and process their experiences both unconsciously and consciously. In this protected space, the child gains insight into their own world, at their own pace, in a safe way.

I work with compassion to build a supportive therapeutic relationship based on trust and understanding, where the child is free to express themselves and make choices. I use a holistic approach to understanding the child, taking into account their developmental stage, relationships, environment, and emotions, while working closely with parents and teachers to meet the child's mental health needs and strengthen outcomes. I follow an integrated theoretical approach, which offers child-centred, 1:1 or group clinical interventions, tailored to meet the developmental needs and family context of the child.
