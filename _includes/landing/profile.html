About Me

My name is Mel, and I am a certified Play Therapist with post-graduate level training and professional registration. My therapeutic practice developed after working for over twenty years in education, with over ten years as a specialist SEND teacher and SEND Coordinator within alternative provision.

I have a specialist interest in neurodiversity and draw on vast experience working with children who are Autistic, ADHD and sensory processing difficulties. Play Therapy is inherently neuro-affirming, working holistically with the child at the centre. I have a deep understanding of child development and am both attachment & trauma informed. I work with children aged 4-13, although please get in touch if your young person is older with SEND. I offer sessions in Worthing and the surrounding areas of West Sussex (South).

Certified Professional with Post-graduate Diploma- Chichester University (Distinction)

I am a registered member of Play Therapy UK (PTUK). This manages the register of Play and Creative Arts Therapists and is accredited by the Professional Standards Authority. It is the largest, most progressive Play Therapy professional organisation both in the UK and internationally.
