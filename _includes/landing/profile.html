About Me

My name is Mel, and I am a qualified practitioner with post-graduate level training and professional registration. My therapeutic practice developed after working for over twenty years in education, with over ten years as a specialist SEND teacher.

I work holistically with the child at the centre, in a way that is both permissive and develops a sense of responsibility. I have a deep understanding of child development and am both attachment & trauma informed. I work with children aged 4-13, although please get in touch if your young person is older with SEND. I offer sessions in Worthing, Shoreham and wider West Sussex (South).

I have a specific interest in neurodiversity and have worked with Autistic & ADHD children and their families for over ten years. Play Therapy offers expression and unconditional acceptance through play and is therefore inherently neuro-affirming.

Certified Professional with Post-graduate Diploma- Chichester University (Distinction)

I am a registered member of Play Therapy UK (PTUK). This manages the register of Play and Creative Arts Therapists and is accredited by the Professional Standards Authority. It is the largest, most progressive Play Therapy professional organisation both in the UK and internationally.
