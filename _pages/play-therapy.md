						<h2 class="h3 mb-3">Individual Play Therapy</h2>
						<p>
							Individual Play Therapy provides weekly one-to-one sessions at a consistent time and place. A clinical hour is charged which includes setting up and clearing down the creative play resources and note taking. A full therapeutic toolkit is provided and sessions are tailored to meet the needs and choices of the child. I can offer sessions either at your child's school or at a dedicated space in Worthing.
						</p>
						<p class="mb-2">Individual Play Therapy can be offered to children experiencing the following difficulties:</p>
						<ul class="mb-0">
							<li>Anxiety, worry or fear</li>
							<li>Low confidence or self-esteem</li>
							<li>Emotional regulation and behavioural struggles</li>
							<li>Family transitions- parental separation, divorce, relocation</li>
							<li>Bereavement, grief or illness</li>
							<li>Friendship difficulties- bullying, self-esteem, asserting self in relationships</li>
							<li>Neurodiversity- understanding and navigating identity</li>
							<li>Early trauma affecting attachment style</li>
							<li>School stress, exam anxiety or school refusal</li>
						</ul>
