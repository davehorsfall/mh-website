You can self-refer or be referred by a school or social worker. I usually respond within two working days. Mel Horsfall Therapy is not an emergency service; in a crisis, please contact 999 and for more immediate support NHS 111 or your GP.

1. **Initial Contact**

   Contact me via email with an outline of your child’s current difficulties and referral
   needs. I will respond with a brief overview of the process and fees with the offer of a
   free phone call.

2. **Free 20-minute phone call**

   A phone conversation to discuss play therapy in more detail in relation to your
   situation. This will give you a chance to ask specific questions and gain further
   information to decide if play therapy is right for your family circumstances.

3. **Parent/ Referrer Consultation**

   If you choose to continue, a longer meeting is arranged to discuss your child’s story
   in more detail to understand how therapy might help your child. A short
   questionnaire and interview are used to develop hopes and expectations for
   therapy. Contracting, Terms and Conditions are shared.

4. **Child Therapy Commences**

   An initial session is held with your child to meet them, share the creative toolkit and
   see if they are happy to attend regular therapy sessions. If they feel they want to
   start therapy, 6 initial sessions are agreed before a short review to check access to
   the toolkit and willingness to attend.

   A further 6 sessions will be agreed.

5. **Regular Reviews**

   At 12 weeks, we will meet to discuss your child’s progress and further presentation.
   The questionnaire completed at referral will be completed again and checked
   against the hopes and expectations for therapy. If further sessions are needed, 6
   more will be planned and reviewed accordingly. If an ending is planned, a
   recommended final block of sessions is completed.
